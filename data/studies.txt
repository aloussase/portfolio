I got my Computer Science degree from Escuela Superior Politecnica del Litoral
in Guayaquil, Ecuador. I was often top of the class and was a teaching assistant
for classes like Systems Programming, Object Oriented Programming and
Distributed Systems. My love for open source put me on the path to become the
president of Kokoa, the university's open source club. Along with the club we
organized an edition of the FLiSoL free software festival in the city of
Guayaquil. I did my undergrad thesis on the performance of distributed tracing.
