I was born in Ecuador but raised in Venezuela and Spain. My childhood was
eventful, but not unlike many others. I am who I am today thanks to my mom, and
for that I will always be grateful. So, who exactly am I? I am an idealist and a
nerd. I am someone who would rather live in their own mind than in the real
world, and so I try to shape my surroundings like my mind. I am someone who loves
learning. Learning about computers, but also about language, philosophy, chess,
bass, videogames, writing; and many more. But who I am is also not set in stone.
I like to think back to who I was a year or five ago and feel as if I can live
someone else's memories in first person. And for that reason I have no idea who
I will be tomorrow. But anyway, this is who I am today.
